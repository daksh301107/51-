About the centre

One of twenty Design Innovation Centres in India, built around clinical partnership

The Ministry of Education's National Initiative for Design Innovation set up 20 Design Innovation Centres and one Open Design School, linked by the National Design Innovation Network. In 2015, Panjab University was chosen to host one at its University Institute of Engineering and Technology.

PU is the hub. Three spokes and two partner hospitals each bring a distinct strength. Ideas selected through competitions and hackathons become technology-driven projects, and many of those become products.

  1. Hub

    Panjab University

    UIET, Sector 25. Design, engineering and AI labs; home of the centre.

  2. Spoke

    CSIR-CSIO

    Scientific instruments and precision engineering.

  3. Spoke

    PEC

    Materials science and transparent ceramics.

  4. Spoke

    HSJIDS

    Dental sciences: aligners, guides and implants.

  5. Clinical partners

    PGIMER & GMCH-32

    Where prototypes are trialled with doctors and patients.
